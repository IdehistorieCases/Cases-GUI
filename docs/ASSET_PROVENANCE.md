- Upstream creator: Quaternius
- Official source: https://quaternius.itch.io/universal-base-characters
- This milestone uses the free Standard edition **Superhero Male** mesh and 65-bone rig.
- Actual downloaded re-distribution: https://github.com/programasweights/avatar/blob/main/public/assets/character.glb
- Downloaded file: `art_source/vendor/quaternius/universal-base-character.glb`
- License text copied from that same source: `QUATERNIUS-LICENSE.txt`, describing **CC0 1.0**.
- Note: Quaternius' general website has also published newer licensing language. Preserve the included terms for these exact assets and verify suitability before republishing raw assets.

## Quaternius Universal Animation Library

- Upstream creator: Quaternius
- Official source: https://quaternius.itch.io/universal-animation-library
- Actual downloaded re-distribution: https://github.com/Seyamalam/blood-league-kickoff/blob/main/public/assets/vendor/quaternius/universal-animation-library.glb

- The generated CASES GLB contains ONLY the seated idle and seated talking actions, not the full library.

## Authored parts

- The formal suit, shirt front, tie, collar, cuffs, chair, and procedural mesh generators were authored specifically for this CASES milestone using Blender Python.
- No third-party finished historical suit meshes were copied.
- V2 adds a jacket profile, bone-aligned sleeves and trousers, a layered waistcoat and shirt, modeled lapels, wrist cuffs, and foot-bound shoes. These are generator-authored geometry and introduce no additional source assets.
- The V3 persona variants reuse that same vendored character and animation source. Their face/build profiles, hair, facial hair, fit parameters, palettes, and senior mediator double-breasted jacket panels are generated by the existing Blender Python pipeline; no new third-party meshes or textures are introduced.
- The generator adds an editable `CASES average-build correction` shape key to the imported source mesh without modifying the vendored file. The exported visible skin is limited to the head/neck and hands; procedural garment profiles define the visible dressed silhouette.

## Base-character selection

- The vendored `universal-base-character.glb` contains the `SuperHero_Male` character mesh and its compatible rig; it does not contain a regular-build male mesh to substitute.
- V2 retains this base because the seated animations are bound to its matching skeleton. The corrective shape key is retained for source-mesh reuse; on this export the torso and arms are covered by generated suit meshes, whose profile parameters define the visible proportions.
- The source `universal-animation-library.glb` supplies only `Sitting_Idle_Loop` and `Sitting_Talking_Loop` to the exported CASES GLB.

## Recommended future improvement

Replace the re-distributed free edition assets with the exact official Standard downloads and store SHA-256 checksums. Consider a regular-proportioned Quaternius body for the final version; this proof used Superhero Male because a properly licensed ready-to-import standalone GLB was available.
